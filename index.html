About

Who I am.

I'm a hardware practitioner based in Berlin with years of experience across the full product lifecycle — from first prototype through EVT, DVT, and PVT to mass production and end-of-life. I currently work as Category Manager in Supply Chain & Hardware at Voi Technology, one of Europe's leading shared micromobility operators, where I manage international supplier sourcing and hardware lifecycle across 150+ cities.

I've watched hardware projects stall at every phase. Sometimes it's a technical problem. More often it's a process problem — a gate that wasn't held honestly, a DFM issue nobody caught early enough, a supplier relationship that isn't working. I'm good at identifying exactly where a project is stuck and what it will take to move it forward.

I have deep experience with Chinese manufacturing partners across Shenzhen, Guangdong, and Jiangsu, and attend the Canton Fair annually. I know what good manufacturing relationships look like, what bad ones cost, and how to tell the difference before you've committed a purchase order.

I wrote The Hardest Hardware Lessons because I watched the same expensive mistakes repeat across hardware teams, year after year — and nobody had written them down. The book sold 45 copies in its first week through word of mouth alone. The problems it describes are real, and widely felt.

I take on a small number of advisory engagements where I can make a genuine difference. If your company is navigating hardware development or China sourcing and needs an experienced outside perspective, I'd like to hear from you.
