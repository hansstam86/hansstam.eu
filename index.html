About

Hans Stam has spent about 20 years in Shenzhen hardware: product development, sourcing and mass production. He moved from Shenzhen to Berlin and still goes back several times a year. He writes as Johan Stam and is the author of The Hardest Hardware Lessons, The Hardest Factory Lessons and GONGBANS. He runs gongbans.com, a catalogue of Shenzhen reference designs for founders, and co-guides Shenzhen Builders Week (December 2026). His line: make the most stupid thing first, then improve it.
